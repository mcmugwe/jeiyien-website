About Us

Who We Are

A Kenyan partner for construction and general supply solutions.

Established in 2016, Jeiyien Trading Company Limited is a Kenyan construction and general supplies company. Our services include road works, residential and commercial building, landscaping, cabro works, interior design and project management.

We believe successful projects start with a clear understanding of the client’s objectives. We work collaboratively, pay attention to quality and keep client satisfaction at the centre of how we plan and deliver our work.

Our slogan, Beyond the Norm, expresses our commitment to going the extra mile and delivering service that meets each client’s needs.

Our Mission

To deliver dependable construction and general supply solutions through efficient work practices, quality workmanship and professionalism, while putting client satisfaction first.

Our Vision

To become a trusted, leading construction and general supplies company, recognised for quality, reliability and excellence in projects that contribute to sustainable development.

Our Services

Integrated construction, infrastructure and general project solutions

Road Construction & Maintenance

We undertake road construction and improvement works, including site preparation, earthworks, paving, road marking, rehabilitation and maintenance.

Building Construction

We deliver residential, commercial and institutional building projects, from new construction to renovation and related building works.

Cabro Paving

We install durable, neatly finished interlocking cabro for driveways, walkways, parking areas and other outdoor spaces.

Water & Drainage Works

We provide water and drainage infrastructure works, including drainage systems, underground water storage cellars, dams and related civil works.

Excavation & Earthworks

We provide site clearance, excavation, trenching and earthmoving for construction, drainage, fibre-optic and infrastructure projects.

Project Management

We coordinate project planning, budgeting, scheduling, site activities and quality control to support efficient project delivery.

Interior Design

We provide interior design solutions that improve the functionality, comfort and appearance of residential and commercial spaces.

Landscaping

We undertake landscaping and outdoor improvement works to create practical, attractive and well-organised external spaces.
